Privacy

Updated October 27, 2026

EspañolEnglishPortuguêsItalianoFrançaisDeutsch

AllThePartyGames is a games app. This note explains what data the app uses and why. It is written so it makes sense at home.

What the phone stores

Your records, progress and settings stay on this device. So does whatever you buy in the shop. Profiles with a name and PIN stay only on this phone, and their PIN does not protect other data on the device. The names and colours of the people you add for group games stay only on this phone. There are no chats, and unfinished games never leave the phone. If you uninstall the app, that save is gone.

Ads

Google AdMob only shows an ad when you choose to, by tapping Watch ad. Google may ask your permission to show you personalized ads; if you do not give it, they are not personalized, and you can change it at any time in Settings. Ads may be for adults. The app does not access the device’s advertising identifier and on iOS it does not ask for permission to track. To deliver, measure, and protect ads, Google may collect and share the IP address (which can estimate approximate location), interactions with the app and ads, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. Data is encrypted in transit.

Purchases

What you buy in the shop is paid through Google Play or the App Store. The store processes payment and tells the app the product, identifier, and purchase status so access can be delivered, restored, or withdrawn. Data is encrypted in transit.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, build advertising profiles, access GPS, or ask for a precise location. There are no surprise loot boxes. There is no chat between players.

Who to ask

The controller of AllThePartyGames and of this data is Brais López Arias (private individual). For a privacy question write to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com. If an ad or a charge does not fit a child, tell us and we will look into it.

Other sites

To know whether there is a connection, the app now and then sends an empty request to Google (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com), which receives the IP address. These are technical HTTPS requests. Google manages ads and purchases; we do not send these services the local profile name, PIN, or your email.

Why and for how long

Non-personalized ads are shown to keep the app free (legitimate interest); personalized ones, only with your consent. Purchases are processed to give you what you ask for (contract). What is stored on the phone stays until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Google keeps its data under its own policies.

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability of your data by writing to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com. Almost everything is only on your phone, and clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it is enough. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Children and families

AllThePartyGames is only for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at minors. When you start, the app asks whether you are 18 or older and, if not, does not let you in. If you are a parent or guardian and want us to review or delete something, write to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com.

Purchase terms

Updated October 21, 2026

EspañolEnglishPortuguêsItalianoFrançaisDeutsch

AllThePartyGames is a collection of party games for adults aged 18 and over. These terms explain what you can pay for in the store and how it works.

What you can buy

Remove ads: a one-time payment that removes the ads between games. Premium: a one-time payment that removes the ads between games and keeps Spicy and Chaos open forever in every game. Without buying anything, Spicy and Chaos open for free after watching a short ad: each day, the first two ads open them for 30 minutes and any later ones for 15 minutes. The current content and price are the ones Google Play or the App Store shows before you confirm; they may vary by country.

Who charges you

Payment is handled by Google Play or Apple, not by us. Refunds are requested from the store under its own rules, and a refund may remove what was bought.

Restoring purchases

Purchases are tied to your store account. If you change phones or reinstall the app, use Restore purchases in the app’s Shop with the same account.

Adults only

The app is for adults aged 18 and over, and purchases must be made by an adult. If a minor has made a purchase, write to us at bryce.lz.as@gmail.com and we will look into it.

Play sensibly

Some games involve alcohol. Nobody has to drink or do a dare: you can play with water or forfeits. Please drink responsibly.

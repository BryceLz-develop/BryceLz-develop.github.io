Delete your account

Updated October 25, 2026

EspañolEnglishPortuguêsItalianoFrançaisDeutsch

How to delete your AllTheGames cloud account and everything it keeps. The account is optional: if you never signed in, nothing of yours is stored outside your phone.

From the app

Open AllTheGames and go to Settings, Cloud account, Delete account. To confirm, the app asks you to sign in again with Google or Apple. It is deleted at once.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot get in, write to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com from the email of your Google or Apple account, with the subject “Delete my AllTheGames account” and your ranking name. We delete it within 30 days at most and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

Your cloud profile (ranking name, avatar, color, coins, cosmetics, level and records), your reserved name, your places in the world ranking and the sign-in account itself. If you delete it from the app, that account’s profile on that phone is deleted too. There are no backups, so it cannot be recovered.

What is not deleted

The passes you bought (remove ads, the month or Premium) belong to your Google Play or App Store account and come back with “Restore purchases”. What you played without an account stays on your phone until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. If you used Apple, deleting from the app also removes the permission you gave the app; otherwise you can remove it yourself in your Apple ID settings.

Privacy

Updated October 6, 2026

EspañolEnglishPortuguêsItalianoFrançaisDeutsch

AllTheGames is a collection of games. This note explains what data the app uses and why. It is written so it makes sense at home.

What the phone stores

Coins, shop passes, records, volume and local profiles stay on this device. We do not have a server that reads matches or chats. If you uninstall, that save is gone.

Optional local profile

You can pick a name and PIN to separate records on this phone. It is not an internet account: it is not synced, cannot be recovered, and the PIN does not protect other data on the device. We do not ask for age, address or a phone number.

Ads

On Android, Google AdMob may show short ads (after a loss, for a hint, or when you tap Watch ad). All requests are treated as child-directed: G-rated, non-personalized, and without permission to access the Android advertising ID. To deliver, measure, and protect ads, Google may collect and share the IP address (which can estimate approximate location), interactions with the app and ads, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. Data is encrypted in transit. Remove Ads, the monthly subscription, or Premium remove ads between games; rewarded ads (for example, to earn coins) stay available only if you choose them. Premium also skips game entry fees.

Purchases

Coin packs, Remove Ads, the monthly pass, and Premium are paid through Google Play or the App Store. The store processes payment and tells the app the product, identifier, and purchase or subscription status so access can be delivered, restored, or withdrawn. We keep on this device the identifiers needed to avoid crediting a consumable pack twice; we do not send them to our own server. Data is encrypted in transit.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, build advertising profiles, access GPS, or ask for a precise location. There are no surprise loot boxes. There is no chat between players.

Who to ask

The controller of AllTheGames and of this data is Brais López Arias (private individual). For a privacy question write to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com. If an ad or a charge does not fit a child, tell us and we will look into it.

Other sites

The word games (Guess the word, Make words and Word chain) check online any word that is not bundled with the app: in Spanish they send the word to rae-api.com, which uses the IP address to limit abuse; in the other languages they send it to Wiktionary (Wikimedia Foundation), which receives the IP address. Flags load from flagcdn.com, whose server receives the IP address and requested image code. To know whether there is a connection, the app now and then sends an empty request to Google (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com), which receives the IP address. These are technical HTTPS requests. Google manages ads and purchases; we do not send these services the local profile name, PIN, or your email. If the app crashes, it sends Sentry (servers in the EU) a technical error report: what failed, the app version and the phone model and system, without IP, profile name or other personal data.

Why and for how long

Ads are always non-personalized, to keep the app free (legitimate interest). Purchases are processed to deliver what you buy (contract). What is stored on the phone stays until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it; Google keeps its data under its own policies.

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability of your data by writing to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com. Since almost everything is stored only on your phone, clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it is usually enough. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Children and families

AllTheGames is for all ages. We do not ask for personal data, ads are always non-personalized and child-appropriate, and real-money purchases are authorized by the Google Play or App Store account, usually an adult’s. If you are a parent or guardian and want us to review or delete something, write to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com.

Purchase terms

Updated 6 October 2026

EspañolEnglishPortuguêsItalianoFrançaisDeutsch

AllTheGames is played on your phone. These terms are about what you pay for in the store. They are written so the whole family can understand them.

What you can buy

Coin packs, removing ads, a monthly subscription that renews automatically, with 40 coins a day and no ads between games, and Premium forever (no ads between games and no entry fee for games). Rewarded ads are still there if the player chooses to watch them. The current content and price are the ones Google Play or the App Store shows you before you confirm; they may vary by country.

Coins

Coins only work inside AllTheGames (entry, hint, undo, continue). They cannot be exchanged for money. If you uninstall, the coins on that phone are lost; paid passes come back with Restore purchases on the same store account.

Who charges you

Google Play or Apple handles the payment, not us. The subscription renews every month until you cancel it from your store account. When you cancel, the benefits last until the end of the period you have already paid for. Refunds are handled by the store and may remove access.

Children

The app is made for playing at home. Purchases are authorised by the store account (almost always an adult). If a charge looks wrong, write to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com.

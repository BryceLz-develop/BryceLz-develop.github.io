Privacy

Updated October 21, 2026

EspañolEnglishPortuguêsItalianoFrançaisDeutsch

AllThePartyGames is a collection of party games for adults aged 18 and over. This note explains what data the app touches and why.

What stays on the phone

The player names you type, the intensity chosen in each game, favourites, store passes, volume and language are stored only on this device. There are no accounts and no server of ours reading your games. If you uninstall the app, all of it is deleted.

Adults only

When you open the app we ask whether you are 18 or older. We only store the answer on the phone; we never ask for a date of birth, ID, address or phone number. Some games involve alcohol and daring content (never explicit): play smart and drink responsibly.

Ads

On Android, Google AdMob shows short ads: now and then between games and, if you choose, one to open Spicy and Chaos for a while. We always request non-personalized ads for an adult audience. Even so, to deliver, measure and protect ads, Google may collect the IP address (which allows an approximate location), interactions with the app and ads, diagnostics and device identifiers. In the European Economic Area and the UK we ask for consent with Google’s form, and you can change it in Settings > Privacy options. If no ad is available, you can play anyway.

Purchases

Remove ads and Premium are paid to Google Play or the App Store. The store processes the payment and tells the app the product and purchase status so it can deliver or restore it. We never see your payment details or send them to a server of ours.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell data, build advertising profiles, or access GPS, the camera, the microphone or your contacts. There is no chat between players and no paid loot boxes.

Who is responsible

The controller of AllThePartyGames and this data is Brais López Arias (private individual). For any privacy question, write to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com.

Other services

The flags in the language picker load from flagcdn.com, which receives the IP and the image code. To check the connection, the app occasionally sends an empty request to Google (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com), which receives the IP. If the app crashes, it sends Sentry (EU servers) a technical error report: what failed, the app version and the phone model and system, with no IP, no player names and no other personal data.

Why and for how long

Ads keep the app free, spicy content included (legitimate interest and, where required, your consent). Purchases are processed to deliver what you buy (contract). What is stored on the phone lasts until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it; Google keeps its data under its own policies.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability of your data by writing to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com. Since almost everything is stored only on your phone, clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it is usually enough. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Minors

AllThePartyGames is not meant for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has used it, write to bryce.lz.as@gmail.com and we will look into it.

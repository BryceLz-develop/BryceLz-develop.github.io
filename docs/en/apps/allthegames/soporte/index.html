Support

Answers to the most common questions. If yours isn’t here, write to me and I’ll reply.

How do I get back what I bought?

Open the Shop and tap «Restore purchases» with the same Google Play account you paid with. Your passes come back: Remove ads, the monthly subscription and Premium. Coins can’t be recovered, because they are only stored on the phone.

Do I lose my coins if I uninstall the app?

Yes. Coins, records and profiles are stored on the phone, not online. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?

In the Shop, tap «Manage subscription», or go to Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. After you cancel, the benefits last until the end of the month you’ve already paid for.

How do I remove the ads?

In the Shop you can buy «Remove ads», the monthly subscription or Premium. Any of them removes the ads between games. Rewarded ads, for example to earn coins, only appear if you choose them.

I’ve forgotten a profile’s PIN

The profile and its PIN only exist on that phone: it isn’t an online account and can’t be recovered. You can create a new profile.

Which games need the internet?

Guess the word, Make words and Word chain check online any words that don’t come with the app, and Flags downloads the flag images. Everything else works offline.

How do I delete my data?

Almost everything is only on your phone. To delete it, open your phone’s settings, go to Apps › AllTheGames › Storage and tap «Clear data» (the names vary a little between phones), or uninstall the app. For any other request about your data, write to me.

Answers to the most common questions. If yours isn’t here, write to me and I’ll reply.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by short ads between games. You can remove the ads or buy Premium if you want, but you don’t need to in order to play.

How do I open Spicy and Chaos?

When you pick Spicy or Chaos in a game, watch a short ad and they stay open for 2 hours in every game. With Premium they are always open.

How do I remove the ads?

In the app’s Shop, buy Remove ads (one payment) or Premium, which also keeps Spicy and Chaos always open. Payment is handled by Google Play.

What does Premium include?

One payment, forever: no ads between games and Spicy and Chaos open in every game.

I changed phones. How do I get my purchases back?

Install the app with the same Google Play account, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases.

Can I add my own prompts or remove the ones we don’t like?

Yes. On each game’s page, tap Phrases to add your own or bring back hidden ones. During a game, tap Don’t show again to hide a card.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no sign-up. Player names, your prompts and your settings are stored only on your phone.

How do I delete my data?

Since everything is stored on the phone, just clear the app’s data in Android settings or uninstall it. Purchases can be restored later with the same account.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the games work offline. You need an internet connection to watch ads and to buy or restore purchases.

Do we have to drink to play?

No. Some games use sips, but you can play with water or forfeits, and nobody has to do anything. Please drink responsibly.

How do I change the language?

In Settings, tap the language and choose English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French or German.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the European Economic Area and the UK, go to Settings > Privacy options.
